MC-WD Wearable Hydrogen Alarm

A meticulously crafted portable H2 detection product. Smaller and lighter than traditional equipment, it supports safety inspections across the entire H2 energy industry chain.

  • Compact and lightweight, clips easily to clothing or belts
  • One-touch operation, simple and quick to use
  • Waterproof, dustproof, and explosion-proof for harsh conditions
  • EMI shielding design for extremely stable detection data
  • Bright LCD display with 3-level audio/visual/vibe alarms
Inquire for Quote

Product Details

The MC-WD Wearable Hydrogen Alarm is a meticulously crafted portable H2 detection product by Metachip. As a new generation of intelligent safety equipment, it breaks the bulky limitations of traditional detectors.

Smaller and lighter than comparable products (weighing only 47g), it can be easily worn on clothing, hats, belts, or pockets. It provides 24/7 continuous dynamic H2 monitoring, serving as a loyal "Safety Guardian" for field workers in the H2 energy industry.

Key Advantages

Ultra-Lightweight

Compact structure and extremely light weight, suitable for long-term wear without burden.

Extended Battery Life

Advanced low-power control technology ensures over 8 hours of continuous operation.

Auto Zero Calibration

Built-in intelligent algorithms eliminate temperature drift, ensuring accurate data.

Authoritative Quality Assurance

Passed national explosion-proof certification and metrological certification for reliability.

Main Applications

H2 Storage Inspection

Personnel carry alarms into storage areas for routine checks, detecting trace leaks in flange seals to ensure safety.

H2 Station Operation

Providing 24/7 protection for station staff, instantly capturing leaks during filling to prevent fire or explosion.

Fuel Cell Vehicles

Ideal for bus and truck technicians, ensuring H2 levels are within safety limits in enclosed maintenance garages.

H2 Logistics Monitoring

Personal safety monitor for drivers during transport and unloading, providing multiple alerts for controlled logistics.

Production Workshop

Assisting inspectors in electrolysis or fossil fuel H2 plants to find blind spots and provide a "Last 1-Meter" life shield.
