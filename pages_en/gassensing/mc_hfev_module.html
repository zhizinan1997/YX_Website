MC-HFEV-02 Multi-purpose H2 Detection Module

High-performance module with built-in intelligent MCU and advanced algorithms, combining high sensitivity of semiconductor sensors with digital integration ease.

  • 2-second rapid response to hydrogen changes, capturing safety hazards in real-time.
  • Wide dynamic range from 2 ppm to 2000 ppm, covering micro-leakage monitoring.
  • Built-in automatic zero-point calibration algorithm for maintenance-free long-term operation.
  • Integrated RS485/RS232 digital communication interfaces, zero threshold for system integration.
  • Automotive-grade design standards, adapted to harsh conditions like vibration and wide temperature ranges.
Download Protocol

Product Details

MC-HFEV-02 is an intelligent gas detection module designed for industrial and automotive applications. Based on high-performance carbon-based semiconductor sensing technology, it integrates high-precision signal conditioning circuits and intelligent processing units.

This module solves common issues like weak signals and interference in traditional analog sensors. Through built-in algorithms, it achieves temperature compensation and automatic baseline calibration. Users can directly obtain accurate concentration data via standard digital interfaces without complex secondary development. Its compact modular design makes it ideal for integrating into Battery Management Systems (BMS), energy storage fire cabinets, and various portable instruments.

Product Advantages

Intelligent Calibration

Built-in adaptive algorithms automatically correct zero drift based on environmental changes, significantly reducing O&M costs over the product lifecycle.

Plug-and-Play

Standard TTL/RS485 digital output supporting Modbus protocol for seamless connection with various PLC and MCU control systems.

High Reliability

Strict EMC design for the circuit, maintaining stable measurement accuracy across a wide temperature range of -40°C to 80°C.

Main Applications

H2 Production & Refueling

Embedded as a core detection unit in field alarms and pipeline monitoring systems to provide real-time feedback on safety status during storage and transport.

Energy Storage Plants

Integrated into fire detection systems of energy storage containers, linking with other sensors to form a multi-dimensional battery safety protection network.

New Energy Vehicles

Installed in fuel cell compartments and passenger cabins, meeting automotive-grade vibration requirements to ensure the safety of hydrogen-fuel vehicles.
