MCHM-1.0 H2 Sensor

Utilizing advanced Metal Oxide Semiconductor (MOS) materials, this long-life, low-cost H2 detection solution is designed for energy storage, metallurgy, and H2 chemical sectors.

  • Excellent heat and corrosion resistance for harsh industrial environments
  • High sensitivity with unique specific response to H2
  • Second-level fast response to capture leak risks in real-time
  • Low power design with exceptional long-term stability
  • Cost-effective manufacturing, ideal for large-scale deployment
Get Datasheet

Product Details

The core of the MCHM-1.0 H2 sensor utilizes high-performance Metal Oxide Semiconductor (MOS) sensing materials. This material exhibits strong adsorption sensitivity and specific selectivity for H2 molecules, effectively shielding against false alarms from other gases.

In terms of structural design, the sensor adopts a standard industrial-grade TO package with a built-in long-lasting physical filter membrane. This design provides strong environmental resistance and blocks dust and oil, significantly extending service life. Combined with a simple driving circuit, it delivers high-precision signal output, making it an ideal core component for industrial IoT and safety monitoring terminals.

Key Advantages

Rapid Response

Nanoscale microstructures on the material surface provide a massive reaction area, triggering immediate resistance changes for fast alerts.

Ultra-Low LOD

Excellent trace gas capturing capability allows early warning at very low H2 concentrations, nipping hazards in the bud.

High Interference Rejection

Unique sensitive electrode formula combined with a physical filter layer effectively suppresses cross-sensitivity to alcohol, CO, etc.

Main Applications

Energy Storage Stations

Monitoring trace H2 during early battery thermal runaway, linked with HVAC and fire systems for a first line of defense.

Metallurgy Industry

Real-time monitoring of H2 in protective atmospheres during annealing and reduction to ensure quality and prevent explosions.

H2 Chemical

Applied in ammonia and methanol manufacturing to monitor leaks around flanges and reactors for plant safety.

H2 Energy Vehicles

Deployed in fuel cell stacks, cylinders, and cabins to sense H2 levels and ensure safe, green travel.
