First Domestic Chip-Printed Semiconductor Sensor Production Line

Total EPC solution for sensor production lines

Case Background

With the explosion of the IoT market, demand for high-performance, low-cost sensors has surged. The customer planned to build an advanced chip-printed semiconductor sensor production line.

Core Requirements & Challenges

A one-stop EPC (Design, Procurement, Construction) service is required, including process design, equipment selection, system integration, and commissioning.

The process is complex, involving micro-nano fabrication technology and requiring extremely high control over environmental cleanliness, temperature, and humidity.

Metachip Solutions

Leveraging deep expertise in micro-nano fabrication, Metachip provided a full suite of solutions from factory planning to process implementation. We customized high-precision printing and sintering equipment and integrated advanced online inspection systems to ensure product consistency and yield.

Results Achieved

The production line succeeded on its first trial run, with all indicators reaching international advanced levels. This significantly shortened the customer's product time-to-market and filled a gap in domestic technology.

Expert Feedback

"The completion of this production line marks a significant step forward for China in the field of high-end sensor manufacturing."

Project Overview

  • Location: Xiangtan, Hunan
  • Industry: Smart Manufacturing
  • Time: 2024
