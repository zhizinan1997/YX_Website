Online Monitoring Systems

Metachip Online Monitoring and Analysis Systems provide real-time solutions for power equipment like transformers, GIS, and switchgear. Utilizing carbon-FET sensing, we achieve a 0-100% VOL ultra-wide dynamic range to capture trace gas anomalies. From single-point monitoring to the "Asset Health Cockpit," we comprehensively enhance maintenance efficiency and safety.
