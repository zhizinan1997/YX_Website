Integrated Sensor Modules

Metachip Integrated Sensor Module series provides customized services and solutions tailored to customer application needs, including device packaging, driver circuits, and signal conversion. Supported gases include hydrogen, methane, VOCs, ammonia, etc., with various ranges and precisions. Each product is meticulously designed one-on-one based on user requirements.
