MCHS-1.0 Palladium Alloy Thin Film H2 Sensor

Based on micro-nano fabricated palladium alloy thin film technology, breaking the dependence of traditional sensors on oxygen. Ideal for premium H2 detection in oxygen-free, high-humidity, and wide-temperature environments.

  • Unique anaerobic detection capability, perfectly adapted to inert atmospheres
  • Ultra-low power design (≤90mW), suitable for portable and wireless applications
  • Wide dynamic range (10ppm ~ 40,000ppm), covering trace to high concentrations
  • Excellent selectivity, with a unique physical adsorption response to hydrogen
  • Highly reliable micro-nano film structure, providing a stable service life of over 5 years
Request Technical White Paper

Product Details

The MCHS-1.0 H2 sensor represents the third generation of solid-state H2 detection technology. Its core employs palladium alloy thin film prepared by precision micro-nano fabrication. Palladium metal possesses a unique "dissolution-expansion" physical property for H2: when hydrogen molecules contact the film surface, they spontaneously dissociate into H atoms and penetrate the lattice, causing a precise and reversible change in the film's resistivity.

Unlike traditional sensors that rely on chemical oxidation, this process is purely physical, so no oxygen is required. This makes MCHS-1.0 indispensable in anaerobic or oxygen-depleted environments such as nuclear power, aerospace, and high-purity gas manufacturing. Meanwhile, proprietary surface nano-coatings effectively isolate sulfides and other interferents, achieving extremely high H2 selectivity.

Key Advantages

Anaerobic Adaptation

Works entirely without oxygen, making it the only ideal choice for H2 leak monitoring in N2-protected, vacuum environments, and high-purity gas pipelines.

Ultra-Wide Range

A single chip can detect from ppm-level trace leaks to the 4% explosive limit (LEL) with excellent linearity and a massive range span.

Micro-Power & Long Life

MEMS thin-film heating power is extremely low (≤90mW), and with no consumable chemicals, it ensures stable operation over a 5-year lifecycle.

Main Applications

ESS Cabinets

Precision monitoring of trace H2 released early during thermal runaway in enclosed battery containers, unaffected by N2 fire suppression systems.

Battery Formation

Environmental safety monitoring in battery formation and grading stages, preventing H2 accumulation to ensure factory safety.

Gas Purification

Detection of trace H2 in oxygen (H2 in O2) at electrolyzer outlets and impurity analysis in various high-purity gases.
