Hydrogen Detection Probe

Built-in self-developed carbon-based FET sensing chip, providing a high-sensitivity, second-level response core detection unit for industrial hydrogen safety.

  • Extreme speed response within 1 second, capturing trace hydrogen concentration fluctuations in real-time.
  • 1 ppm ultra-low detection limit for excellent early leakage warning.
  • Standard TO package supporting various gas intake schemes including diffusion and pump suction.
  • Equipped with efficient long-life filters to effectively resist chemical impurity interference.
  • Micro-nano semiconductor fabrication ensures high consistency and long-term stability.
Get Specifications

Product Details

Metachip Hydrogen Detection Probe is a core module developed specifically for industrial OEM integration and high-precision monitoring. At its heart is a leading carbon-based FET sensor chip. Unlike traditional sensors, it is manufactured using advanced semiconductor micro-nano processes, ensuring extreme consistency in sensitivity and linearity across every probe produced.

To adapt to diverse industrial environments, the probe uses a standard industrial TO structure. This design not only guarantees excellent physical protection but also makes it perfectly compatible with various diffusion detectors or pump suction sampling systems. Diverse electrical interface designs significantly reduce integration difficulty for terminal manufacturers.

Core Advantages

Rapid Dynamic Response

Thanks to the high-efficiency electronic transmission of carbon-based chips, signal feedback is completed within 1 second, leading the industry.

Precision Detection

The detection limit reaches 1 ppm, accurately quantifying extremely weak hydrogen permeation to provide data support for high-standard security.

Excellent Anti-interference

Built-in specially designed physical filters effectively filter out common gas components and interfering odors in the air, focusing only on hydrogen.

主要应用

氢化工安全监控

集成于化工厂内的各类分析仪表或巡检机器人，实时探测反应釜周边及管道接口处的泄漏风险。

冶金还原气氛监测

在各种需要使用氢气作为还原保护气体的热处理流程中，精准控制氢气比例，保障工艺质量与安全。

电化学储能消防

广泛应用于锂电池舱热失控早期预警。通过捕捉电池释气的关键组分——氢气，提前发出火灾警报。
