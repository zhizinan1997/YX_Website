    Job Responsibilities:

    Senior Hardware Development Engineer (10k-20k)

    Job Responsibilities:

    1. Responsible for hardware circuit design, schematic drawing, and layout submission and debugging of biological and chemical sensor application products.

    2. Design application circuits and complete design plans according to customer or project requirements, and output design plan demonstration reports.

    3. Complete new product system debugging, hardware testing, comprehensive effect debugging and calibration, output corresponding reports, and output a full set of engineering technical documents on time and with quality.

    4. Responsible for organizing company hardware and software product testing, production conversion, and early maintenance guidance, and assisting in handling technical problems in customer and production.

    4. Proficient in welding processes and common R&D equipment. Familiar with circuit knowledge, proficient in analog or digital circuit design, familiar with various common components, able to build common circuits or proficient in C language, able to perform underlying software development and debugging for microcontrollers and embedded systems.
