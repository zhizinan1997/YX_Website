Share the development opportunities of the hydrogen energy industry and work together to open a new chapter in sensing.

Agent Recruitment

Hunan Metachip is a national high-tech enterprise incubated by a research team from Peking University, dedicated to the R&D and manufacturing of advanced biological and chemical sensor products. The high-sensitivity hydrogen sensor and its detector developed by the company are widely used in nitrogen-hydrogen tracer leak detection, electrochemical energy storage, and hydrogen energy vehicles. To better expand the market and share the opportunities of hydrogen energy industry development with more distributors and integrators, Metachip is now launching a partner recruitment campaign for the company's hydrogen safety series products.

Agent Recruitment Scope

(1) Recruitment Area: Provincial-level, provincial capital cities, municipalities directly under the central government, and subordinate prefecture-level cities nationwide.

(2) Industry Scope: Companies engaged in sales or system integration in fields such as industrial safety, fire safety, environmental protection, instrumentation, automation equipment, gas detection/analysis equipment, laboratory equipment, HVAC, etc.
