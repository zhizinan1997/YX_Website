Company Profile

Metachip Technology

Hunan Metachip Technology Co., Ltd. (Metachip) originates from over 20 years of research by a top-tier team from Peking University, specializing in the R&D and manufacturing of advanced sensing products. We are dedicated to developing novel carbon-based FET sensing technologies for next-generation, high-performance integrated sensing chips.

Sensing serves as the vital interface between information technology and the physical world. Our advanced products are essential components of modern industrial evolution, impacting various sectors of global technology. Metachip strives to explore the limitless possibilities of sensing alongside our partners.

Core Technology

Our core material—Carbon Nanotubes (CNTs)—is recognized by the ITRS as the most promising candidate for extending Moore's Law. With ultra-thin dimensions and high mobility, CNT-based sensors offer superior sensitivity and integration, enabling precise detection for various trace targets.
