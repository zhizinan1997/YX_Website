Collaboration Modes

  • Joint Project Development

    As a technical partner, Metachip works with you to design plans and write bids. After successful project approval, we handle the engineering implementation, while you focus on core scientific research.

  • IP co-creation

    Providing technical support for your research projects, assisting in overcoming process challenges, and helping with patent applications and the "Experimental Methods" section of papers.

  • Laboratory Co-construction

    For research groups needing special test platforms or pilot production lines, Metachip provides EPC turnkey services from design and equipment procurement to system integration.

Case Studies

EPC for Domestic's First Chip-Printed Semiconductor Gas Sensor Line (Yueyang, Hunan)

The client required a world-leading methane sensor manufacturing solution and production equipment. After multiple evaluations, they chose to cooperate with Metachip.

Based on years of carbon-based sensing research, Metachip provided a full EPC service for the methane sensor production line, driving innovation in the client's production line, products, and solutions.

Construction and acceptance were completed in 6 months, providing the client with a production capacity of 8 million sensors per year.
