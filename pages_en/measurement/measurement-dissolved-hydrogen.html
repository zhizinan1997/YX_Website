"Golden Indicator" for Fault Warning

Among the gases generated from internal transformer faults, hydrogen (H₂) is the earliest produced and fastest to diffuse. Whether the fault is caused by partial discharge, arcing, or overheating, hydrogen is almost always generated. Through single hydrogen monitoring, you can cover more than 90% of early latent faults at the lowest cost, transforming from "passive repair" to "active prevention."

Micro-electro-mechanical Hydrogen Sensing Technology

Traditional gas chromatography requires complex oil-gas separation devices and carrier gas cylinders, making maintenance tedious. Metachip uses independently developed MEMS hydrogen sensing chips, which are directly immersed in oil for measurement (or through a gas-permeable membrane). They have extremely high selectivity for hydrogen and are not subject to cross-interference from other gases in the oil (such as CO, CH4), achieving truly maintenance-free online monitoring.
