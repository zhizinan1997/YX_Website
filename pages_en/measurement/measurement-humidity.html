Invisible Key Metrics

Humidity not only affects product quality and shelf life, but is also directly related to the compliance and safety of the production environment. In the pharmaceutical, food, and electronics manufacturing sectors, precise humidity control is the first line of defense against mold, static electricity, and chemical denaturation.

Explore life science applications

Benchmark for Sensing Technology

Since inventing the world's first thin-film capacitive humidity sensor in 1973, our technology has been known for stability and precision. Unique heated probe technology and chemical purging allow our sensors to maintain excellent long-term stability even in harsh environments with high humidity, condensation, or drift caused by chemical contaminants.
