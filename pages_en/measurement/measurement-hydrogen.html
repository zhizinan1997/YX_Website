Hydrogen (H₂) Measurement and Monitoring

As the future carrier of clean energy, hydrogen gives us unlimited possibilities. At the same time, its flammable and explosive characteristics also pose severe safety challenges. Metachip provides precise monitoring solutions for the entire industry chain from hydrogen production to application.

Small Molecules, Big Challenges

Hydrogen is the gas with the smallest molecules and the fastest diffusion, capable of penetrating ordinary sealing materials. In enclosed industrial environments or high-pressure storage and transportation processes, even minute leaks can rapidly accumulate to reach explosive limits. We need monitoring methods that are more sensitive and faster than traditional ones.

Learn industrial safety standards

Carbon-based Nano-sensing Technology

Metachip uses revolutionary Carbon Nanotube (CNT) sensing chips. Unlike metal oxide sensors that require heating, CNT chips work at room temperature with extremely low power consumption (microwatt level) and have unique selective adsorption capabilities for hydrogen, fundamentally solving the high power consumption and cross-interference issues of traditional sensors.

Explore CNT sensing principles

Key Application Scenarios

Guarding safety in all dimensions, from energy to monitoring

Hydrogen Energy Industry Chain

Providing full-link leakage monitoring and concentration analysis from hydrogen production stations and refueling stations to hydrogen fuel cell vehicles.

Energy Storage Battery Safety

Monitoring trace hydrogen released during early thermal runaway of lithium batteries, achieving fire warning earlier than temperature and smoke sensors.
