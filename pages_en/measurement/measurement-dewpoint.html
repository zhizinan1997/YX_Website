Redefining Drying Process Control

Dew point is a key metric for measuring the dryness of a gas. Unlike relative humidity, dew point values are not affected by temperature and can truly reflect the absolute water content in a gas. For applications such as compressed air systems, plastic drying, and ozone generators, precise dew point monitoring means higher energy efficiency and lower product waste rates.

Thin-film capacitive sensor specifically designed for industrial drying environments. Unique automatic calibration eliminates long-term drift, and the anti-condensation design allows it to recover quickly after water splashes or high humidity, ensuring measurement continuity and accuracy.
