"A great scientific achievement is born from countless experiments and explorations. Metachip is committed to building a bridge for cutting-edge scientific research through our professional engineering capabilities, allowing the fruits of scientists' wisdom to be transformed into reality faster."

Cooperation Models

Joint Project Development

Metachip acts as a technical partner to co-design solutions and write bids. After successful project initiation, we are responsible for engineering implementation, while you focus on core scientific issues.

Co-creating IP

Provide technical support for your projects, assist in overcoming process difficulties, and have our engineers assist in writing patent applications and "Experimental Methods" sections in papers.

Joint Lab Construction

For groups needing to build special test platforms or pilot lines, Metachip provides EPC turnkey services from design and equipment procurement to system integration.

Success Story

Domestic's First Chip-based Printed Semi. Gas Sensor Line EPC

Location: Yueyang, Hunan

Customer required a world-leading methane sensor manufacturing solution & line. Based on years of CNT sensing research, Metachip provided a full EPC service. The line was completed and accepted within 6 months, achieving an annual capacity of 8 million sensors for various types.
