Featured Project Cases

Custom Device Development

#Micro-nanoFabrication #YieldOptimization #TopGateDevice

Background: A top domestic microelectronics research institute needed high-performance top-gate FETs.
Challenge: Strict channel length control (3-5um) and high switching ratio (>10⁵).
Solution: We grew high-quality HfO2 gate dielectric using ALD, combined with dry-wet composite lift-off, achieving perfect micron-scale pattern retention and significantly improved yield.

Flexible Thin-film Strain Sensor

#FlexibleElectronics #Non-destructivePeeling #PrecisionPatterning

Background: A university material team was developing wearable sensors and faced micro-nano fabrication bottlenecks on flexible substrates.
Solution: Used ultrafast laser "cold processing" to avoid thermal damage, with gradient evaporation for enhanced electrode adhesion, and non-destructive device transfer via TMAH wet etching.
