Metachip Awarded "National High-tech Enterprise" Certification!
Leading with Carbon-based Sensing Technology, Safeguarding Health and Energy Safety

2025-04-03

Dear Customers and Partners:  

Recently, Hunan Metachip Technology Co., Ltd. has successfully passed the National High-tech Enterprise Certification, relying on its core independent intellectual property rights, ability to transform scientific and technological achievements, and leading R&D organization and management level! This honor is not only an authoritative recognition of Metachip's technical strength, but also marks that our innovation achievements in the field of carbon-based materials and sensing technology have entered a new stage of industrialization.  


                    


1. Innovation-driven, Leading the Industrialization of Carbon-based Sensing Technology

As a pioneer in the field of carbon-based sensing technology in China, Metachip has always taken "benefiting mankind with invention" as its mission. Relying on the scientific research accumulation of the Peking University research team and the company's unique carbon nanotube sensing platform, we provide brand-new solutions for ultra-high sensitivity biological and chemical detection scenarios. We have developed and produced a series of products such as biosensing chips, gas sensing chips, sensing application systems and instruments, and built a full-chain technology platform covering biomedicine, hydrogen energy safety, environmental monitoring and other fields.


Core Achievement 1: Biosensing Chips, the "Sharp Eyes" of Precision Medicine

  • Disease Screening: The self-developed carbon-based biosensing chip, with atomic-level sensitivity and rapid response capabilities, has been applied to early screening of liver cancer and Alzheimer's disease, providing precise data support for clinical diagnosis.

  • Virus Detection: During the COVID-19 pandemic, chip technology achieved ultra-fast detection of viral nucleic acids, assisting public health prevention and control.

  • Food Safety: The biosensing workstation can efficiently detect pathogenic microorganisms and toxins in food, providing reliable guarantees for rapid food safety testing.  


                      Metachip Carbon-based Bio-Platform                                                 Metachip Carbon-based Bio-Package Chip                                              Metachip Biosensing Workstation


Core Achievement 2: Hydrogen Sensors, Escorting the Safe Development of the Hydrogen Energy Industry

  • Hydrogen Detection: The world's first carbon-based hydrogen detector (LD-H2) has passed national explosion-proof and metrological certification, capable of detecting 1ppm hydrogen within 1 second, with internationally leading performance.

  • Tracer Leak Detection: Successfully developed a nitrogen-hydrogen tracer leak detector based on carbon-based sensing chips, with a leak detection rate as low as 5*10-7mbar · l/s, comparable to helium detection performance, providing new solutions for the industrial leak detection field.

  • Scenario Implementation: Products have been widely used in hydrogen energy vehicles, hydrogen refueling stations, electrochemical energy storage systems, industrial tracer leak detection and other fields, building a full life cycle safety monitoring system. 


                      Metachip Handheld Hydrogen Detector                                                 Metachip Vehicle-mounted Hydrogen Sensing Module                                           Metachip Nitrogen-Hydrogen Tracer Detector


2. Deep Integration of Industry, Academia and Research, Accelerating Technology Transformation

Metachip adheres to "technology as the foundation, application as the core", and deeply cooperates with Peking University Carbon-based Electronics Research Center, Hunan Advanced Sensing and Information Technology Research Institute and other institutions to form a "R&D-Transformation-Application" closed loop:

  • Industrialization Benchmark: Jointly conquered the batch preparation process of carbon-based sensors, completed the leap from laboratory prototypes to engineering products, and the carbon-based hydrogen detector (LD-H2) passed national explosion-proof and metrological certification, entering the stage of large-scale application.

  • Authoritative Recognition: Technical achievements have appeared many times at top academic conferences and industry exhibitions, and cooperation with leading hydrogen fuel cell engine companies to carry out field tests, optimizing the stability and reliability of products in complex environments, laying the foundation for industrialization.


                                                                                     Metachip cooperates with customers to carry out Tianshan hydrogen fuel cell engine verification experiments


3. Future Outlook: Empowering Industries with Technology

Winning the National High-tech Enterprise certification marks a new starting point for Metachip's high-quality development.

Facing the future, we will keep up with the wave of scientific and technological innovation, continue to deepen R&D investment, and drive productivity innovation with core technology breakthroughs. By continuously strengthening the original advantages of carbon-based sensing technology, deploying in medical health monitoring, biological molecule detection, hydrogen energy safety monitoring, industrial intelligent early warning and other fields, we are committed to providing more valuable sensing solutions for national strategic emerging industries.
