Solving the Dilemma of Cost and Precision, Nitrogen-Hydrogen Tracer Leak Detection Technology Breaks the Industrial Leak Detection Deadlock

2025-09-16

That order from a "Major Client", is it the opportunity you dreamed of, or the last straw that breaks your profits?

This depends on whether you have the matching quality inspection capabilities.

One of our clients, an acrylic experimental chamber supplier, once faced such a life-and-death test. After receiving a high-standard order from Huawei, they found that traditional air leak testing and bubble methods could no longer meet the requirements at all.

They knew the products had leaks, but couldn't find where the leak points were. And outsourcing a high-precision helium inspection, the quotation was as high as nearly a thousand yuan, which made the originally considerable profit negligible.

This case sharply points out a widespread problem: When customer requirements increase, while your detection capabilities remain in place, how will you respond? Is it to give up the order, or sacrifice profit?

Breaking the Deadlock: We Found the Neglected "Third Way"

For a long time, there seemed to be only two options for leak detection in the industrial field: either choose the "Gold Standard" helium inspection and endure its high cost; or choose traditional methods and tolerate their lack of precision.

But in Metachip's view, the significance of technological innovation lies in breaking this either-or dilemma. We found that between the expensive "Gold Standard" and backward traditional methods, there is a huge market gap and industrial demand. This demand is a universal leak detection technology that can guarantee high precision while controlling costs within a reasonable range.

Our answer is Nitrogen-Hydrogen Tracer Leak Detection Method.

This forward-looking solution has recently won unanimous recognition from expert judges in the expert-studded 2025 "Maker China" High-end Scientific Instrument Equipment Special Competition, and won the Excellence Award.

Masterpiece: Metachip MC-TD-01 Nitrogen-Hydrogen Tracer Leak Detector

This honor motivates us to faster translate solutions into reliable products that can create value for customers. Today, we officially bring you — Metachip MC-TD-01 Carbon-based Nitrogen-Hydrogen Tracer Leak Detector.

Hardcore Strength, Performance Seen to be Believed

The workflow of MC-TD-01 is designed to be extremely simple: use safe, non-flammable nitrogen-hydrogen mixture gas (5% H₂ + 95% N₂) to pressurize the workpiece, and then hold our ergonomic sniffing handle to scan along key sealing positions. Tiny leak points, revealed in one second.

A thousand theories are not as good as seeing it once on site.
