Committed to Hydrogen Safety, Metachip LD-H2 Hydrogen Detector Successfully Passed National Explosion-proof and Metrology Certification!

2024-11-27


Recently, Hunan Metachip Technology Co., Ltd.'s LD-H2 hydrogen detector successfully passed the national explosion-proof and metrology certification, obtaining the CNEX explosion-proof certificate and the calibration certificate from the China Institute of Metrology. This means that Metachip's hydrogen detector products have met relevant national and international standards, and the product detection accuracy has reached an internationally leading level.


  


 

Keeping pace with technological innovation, building internationally leading hydrogen detection products

Metachip adheres to the development concept of "benefiting mankind with invention", relies on the technical advantages of the carbon-based chip research team of Peking University, and continues to invest in R&D in the field of gas sensing and instrumentation. Benchmarking against the world's most cutting-edge gas detection technology, after several years of hard work, Metachip successfully developed the world's first carbon-based chip hydrogen detector LD-H2, and its product performance is leading globally.


   


 

Escorting national hydrogen energy development, building a world-leading hydrogen detection brand

China's hydrogen energy development has huge prospects. According to the "China Hydrogen Energy Industry Infrastructure Development Blue Book (2016)", it is estimated that by 2025, the number of hydrogen fuel cell vehicles in China will exceed 100,000, the number of hydrogen refueling stations will exceed 1,000, the green hydrogen production capacity will approach 1 million tons, and the scale of the hydrogen energy industry will reach one trillion yuan. In November 2024, the 12th meeting of the Standing Committee of the 14th National People's Congress voted to pass the "Energy Law of the People's Republic of China". Hydrogen energy was explicitly included in the energy management system for the first time, and the prospects for the development of the hydrogen energy industry are clearer.

Safety is the foundation of the development of the hydrogen energy industry. As the application scale of hydrogen energy in transportation, energy storage, industry and other fields continues to expand, the challenges to hydrogen energy safety are also increasing day by day. It is now generally believed in the industry that against the background of increasingly rich hydrogen energy application scenarios and national policies allowing integrated hydrogen production and refueling stations to no longer be limited to chemical parks, it is crucial to strengthen hydrogen energy safety assurance and achieve safety protection monitoring and early warning for the entire industry chain.

Metachip LD-H2 hydrogen detector uses high-sensitivity carbon-based sensing chips, which have sensitivity and response speed unmatched by traditional sensing chips. It can detect trace hydrogen with a concentration of less than 1ppm within 1 second, and is suitable for all links of hydrogen energy production, storage, transportation, refueling and use, helping users accurately locate the leakage source. In addition, the IoT function of the LD-H2 hydrogen detector supports hydrogen-related enterprises to build a comprehensive safety system, helping enterprises establish a hydrogen energy safety early warning system and effectively improving the safety of hydrogen-related places.



Explosion protection is the pillar of hydrogen energy safety, and metrology is the cornerstone of hydrogen energy development. Metachip adheres to the policy of "scientific, standardized, safe and efficient", and is committed to in-depth research on hydrogen energy detection technology and strengthening of equipment detection capabilities. In the three aspects of sensor chips, circuit design and sensing algorithms, the company continues to achieve technological innovation breakthroughs, and provides customers with high-standard and highly reliable hydrogen detection products with a strict quality control system. The passing of the national explosion-proof and metrology certification for this product not only marks that the company's products have officially entered the track of standardized and normalized development, but also means further improvement of the company's product competitiveness.

Looking forward to the future, Metachip will continue to provide reliable products for hydrogen energy customers with professional technology and high standards, and constantly expand product functions according to customer needs, contributing to the safe development of China's new hydrogen energy productive forces.

About National Explosion-proof and Metrology Certification

National Quality Supervision and Inspection Center for Explosion-proof Electrical Products (CNEX) is a national safety production explosion-proof electrical testing and inspection center authorized by the State Administration of Work Safety. It is the most authoritative explosion-proof certificate review institution in China, possessing authority, independence, impartiality and technicality. Without a "China Explosion-proof" certificate or using explosion-proof products from other countries/regions does not meet legal and regulatory requirements in China.

National Institute of Metrology, China, affiliated to the State Administration for Market Regulation, is the highest national metrology scientific research center and a national statutory metrology technical institution; The calibration certificate of the National Institute of Metrology, China is a certification document issued by the National Institute of Metrology, China, used to prove that the calibrated instrument, equipment or system meets certain accuracy and reliability requirements. Obtaining the calibration certificate of the National Institute of Metrology, China means that the calibrated item meets national or international standard requirements, has reliable measurement capabilities, and can be widely recognized and applied in relevant fields.
