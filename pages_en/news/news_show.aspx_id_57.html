Hardcore Strength, Another Honor! Metachip Wins 1st Place in "Dongsheng Cup" Q3 Hydrogen Special Finals!

2025-09-01

Recently, the highly anticipated 2025 13th "Dongsheng Cup" International Entrepreneurship Competition Q3 Daxing International Hydrogen Energy Demonstration Zone Hydrogen Energy Special Quarter Finals came to a successful conclusion. In this fierce competition gathering top hydrogen energy innovation projects from all regions of the country, Hunan Metachip Technology Co., Ltd., with its self-developed "Carbon-based Hydrogen Sensing Technology" project, won high recognition from the judges and experts, and won the first place in this quarter's competition!


  


"Dongsheng Cup": World-class Innovation Arena

"Dongsheng Cup" International Entrepreneurship Competition is a top international entrepreneurship event under the joint guidance of multiple government departments such as the Beijing Municipal Human Resources and Social Security Bureau and the Beijing Municipal Science & Technology Commission. Since its inception in 2013, it has attracted over 16,000 entrepreneurial projects globally, with total financing of winning projects exceeding 20 billion yuan, making it a veritable "Unicorn Cradle" and innovation vane.

This hydrogen energy special quarter finals focused on the energy material track under the national "Dual Carbon" strategy, and had extremely rigorous considerations for the project's technological foresight, market application value, and industrialization potential. Standing out on such a stage fully proves Metachip's leading technological position and broad prospects in the field of hydrogen safety monitoring.


 


Carbon-based "Hydrogen Core": Guarding the Safety Lifeline of Hydrogen Industry

The hydrogen energy industry is booming, but "safety" is always the cornerstone of its healthy development. The colorless, odorless, flammable, and explosive characteristics of hydrogen pose extremely high requirements for leak monitoring.

Metachip's core technology — "Carbon-based Hydrogen Sensing Technology", came to solve this core pain point. Our self-developed carbon nanotube transistor sensor acts as a "sentry" and "electronic nose" tailored for the hydrogen energy industry, possessing two core advantages:

• Extreme Sensitivity: Can accurately detect hydrogen concentration of one part per million (PPM) in the air within 1 second, achieving "zero tolerance" for minor leaks.

• Instant Response: Nanosecond-level response speed can issue a warning the moment a leak occurs, buying precious time for emergency disposal.


Relying on its excellent performance, low power consumption, and high reliability, our technology can be widely applied in hydrogen production, storage, transportation, refueling stations, and fuel cells, providing the most solid underlying technical guarantee for hydrogen safety. This victory is the best proof that our technical solution can effectively empower the industry.


Moving Forward with Honor, Keeping Original Aspiration

Winning the first place this time is encouragement, but also a new starting point.

Metachip will continue to uphold the concept of "Technology-based, Innovation-driven", constantly exploring in the vast universe of carbon-based sensing. We firmly believe that advanced sensing technology is an important cornerstone of the digital age and energy revolution.

In the future, we will continue to work hand in hand with industry colleagues to contribute "Metachip" power to the national "Dual Carbon" goal and the booming development of the hydrogen industry!
