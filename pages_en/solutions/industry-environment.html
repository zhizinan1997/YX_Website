Atmospheric Environment Monitoring

High-precision gridded air quality monitoring terminals, helping to depict the digital blueprint of green cities.

Gridded Air Quality Station
Industrial Fugitive Emission Monitoring
Carbon Neutrality & Greenhouse Gas Monitoring
Smart Meteorology & Research-grade Sensing

Micro-gridded Air Quality Station

Traditional monitoring stations are bulky and expensive. Metachip provides micro-monitoring terminals that rely on highly stable, anti-interference carbon-based MEMS sensors to achieve high-precision detection of key indicators such as PM2.5, SO2, NO2, and O3. Due to their lightweight size and support for solar power, they can be easily deployed on urban street light poles or park sections, achieving street-level "gridded" precise profiling.

Industrial Park Fugitive Emission Monitoring

In industrial parks such as petrochemicals and manufacturing, monitoring fugitive emissions outside "chimneys" is a difficult point in environmental protection work. Metachip modules support rapid capture of low-concentration VOCs and H2S. Combined with wind speed and direction meters and park topology mathematical models, the system can track pollutant diffusion paths in real-time, helping management departments quickly locate leakage points or illegal discharge sources.

Carbon Neutrality & Greenhouse Gas Online Monitoring

Achieving the "dual carbon" goal is inseparable from finding out the bottom line of carbon emissions. Metachip's high-precision NDIR infrared sensor modules (such as CO2 and CH4 sensors) have extremely low span drift and extremely long calibration-free cycles. Whether for urban traffic carbon emission flux monitoring or research-grade carbon sink observation for wetlands and forests, Metachip can provide reliable underlying hardware support.

Smart Meteorology & Multi-dimensional Sensor Fusion

True environmental monitoring is the fusion of multi-dimensional data. Metachip's integrated environmental perception module can integrate temperature, humidity, air pressure, and various gas sensing on a single chip. Through low-pass filtering and environmental dynamic compensation algorithms, the sensor can output high-confidence data under varying climatic conditions, empowering frontier fields such as smart agriculture, unmanned driving, and aviation safety.
