Smart Power Safety

Real-time online monitoring for transformers, GIS, and switchgear, safeguarding the operation of new power systems.

Online Monitoring of Transformers and Dissolved Gases in Oil

Industry Pain Points

Large oil-immersed transformers face irreversible risks of insulation material aging under long-term high-voltage and high-heat environments. Traditional inspections struggle to detect internal latent faults, which once failed, can trigger major power grid accidents.

Gas Generation Mechanism

When insulation oil or paper undergoes micro-discharge or overheating internally, their hydrocarbon molecular chains break and recombine, making Hydrogen (H2) the earliest and most sensitive characteristic gas, known as the "barometer" of faults.

Smart Online Monitoring

Surpassing the lag of traditional manual sampling, achieving 24/7 all-weather monitoring. Through hydrogen concentration trend analysis, the system can issue warnings weeks before a fault evolves into a disaster, shifting from "passive repair" to "proactive maintenance."

Core Technology

Equipped with self-developed carbon-based MEMS chips, utilizing electronic band effects to detect hydrogen molecules. With three major advantages: maintenance-free, long life, and anti-interference, it is the ideal choice for transformer lifecycle management.

GIS Composite Electrical Equipment and SF6 Gas Monitoring

For GIS (Gas Insulated Switchgear), we provide high-precision SF6 leakage alarm and pressure monitoring solutions. Our system not only detects extremely low concentrations of SF6 leakage in real-time, ensuring environmental safety, but also seamlessly connects with substation integrated automation systems through digital interfaces, achieving 24/7 control of equipment operation status.

Smart Temperature Measurement for High-Voltage Cables and Switchgear

Cable joints and switchgear contacts are common hotspots in power systems. Metachip's wireless, passive temperature measurement system completely solves the insulation and power harvesting challenges in high-voltage environments. The system monitors contact temperatures in real-time and identifies abnormal heating trends through smart algorithms, triggering warnings before power outages occur.
