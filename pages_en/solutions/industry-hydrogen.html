Metachip Hydrogen Production Station Safety Solutions

Based on Metachip's world-leading autonomous and controllable carbon-based chip technology, we provide hydrogen concentration detection, customized system development, technical support, and services for hydrogen production scenarios such as water electrolysis, natural gas hydrogen production/blending, ammonia cracking, methanol reforming, hydrogen pipelines, skid-mounted stations, and hydrogen-related laboratories.

Metachip Hydrogen Refueling Station Safety Solutions

Based on Metachip's world-leading autonomous and controllable carbon-based chip technology, we provide hydrogen concentration detection, customized system development, technical support, and services for scenarios such as hydrogen refueling stations, discharge columns, hydrogen fuel cell vehicles and system test benches, combined heat and power (CHP), and hydrogen cylinder rooms.

Metachip Hydrogen Fuel Vehicle Solutions

Based on Metachip's world-leading autonomous and controllable carbon-based chip technology, we provide automotive-grade hydrogen sensors and safety monitoring modules for hydrogen fuel cell commercial and passenger vehicles. The system can monitor hydrogen leakage in fuel cell systems and hydrogen storage systems in real-time, and perform high-speed data communication with the Vehicle Control Unit (VCU), achieving millisecond-level responses and multi-level safety warnings, ensuring the intrinsic safety of hydrogen vehicles under various harsh operating conditions.

Metachip Hydrogen Pipeline Network and Domestic Hydrogen Use Safety Solutions

With the advancement of the "Hydrogen into Every Home" demonstration project, Metachip provides full-lifecycle hydrogen safety monitoring solutions for long-distance hydrogen pipelines, urban gas-hydrogen blending networks, and end-user domestic hydrogen environments. Utilizing high-sensitivity carbon-based sensing technology, the system can precisely identify trace leakages in the pipeline network and provide real-time positioning. At domestic hydrogen terminals, it offers millisecond-level proprietary detection feedback, thoroughly addressing the "last hundred meters" safety pain point after hydrogen energy enters the civilian sector, safeguarding the energy safety of thousands of households.

Metachip Trace Hydrogen Detection Solutions

For fields with extreme requirements for hydrogen monitoring sensitivity, such as the semiconductor industry, dissolved gas analysis (DGA) in power transformer oil, and precision laboratory scientific research, Metachip has launched trace-level hydrogen detection solutions. Relying on self-developed carbon-based sensing chips, this solution can achieve capture at extremely low concentrations down to 1ppm, with excellent zero-point stability and resistance to cross-gas interference. Its high-precision real-time feedback provides solid underlying data support for the intrinsic safety of precision manufacturing and large-scale power assets.

Metachip Hydrogen Energy Safety Management Platform

Based on sensor IoT and digital cloud technology, Metachip hydrogen safety solutions can achieve real-time monitoring of hydrogen leakage status and promptly push early warning information to customers. Meanwhile, through intelligent mining and analysis of massive monitoring data, an early warning system and comprehensive early warning model are constructed. The platform can achieve safety remote diagnosis, information mutual feedback, and identification for multiple scenarios of hydrogen production, storage, transportation, refueling, and use, effectively guiding on-site risk control.

Value & Advantages

01

Full-Link Digital Centralized Monitoring

Integrate sensor data from the entire hydrogen production, storage, transportation, and refueling industry chain to establish a unified hydrogen safety asset portrait. Through a visual large screen, synchronous mapping of physical and digital spaces is achieved, ensuring every risk point is under control, transitioning from "passive response" to "warning-driven."

02

Multi-dimensional Three-dimensional Emergency Linkage

Break the communication barriers between sensors, cloud platforms, and on-site operators. Once the warning threshold is triggered, the system immediately starts a second-level response mechanism: automatically cutting off the gas source, starting forced ventilation, and pushing warnings to mobile devices in milliseconds, building an interlocking "human-machine-object" safety closed-loop protection network.

03

High Compatibility and Flexible Expansion

Utilizing advanced architecture and standardized interface design, it supports seamless access for various communication methods such as NB-IoT, 4G, and LoRa. The system possesses strong horizontal expansion capabilities, supporting concurrent access for tens of thousands of points, and can be seamlessly integrated into existing enterprise ERP, EHS, or MES systems.
