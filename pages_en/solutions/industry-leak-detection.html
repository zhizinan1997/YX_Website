Lithium Battery and PACK Sealing Detection

The safety and lifespan of lithium batteries are highly dependent on excellent sealing. Metachip's high-sensitivity tracer gas (such as hydrogen, helium) monitoring modules are specifically designed for lithium battery PACK and cell production lines. Through a combination of differential pressure and tracer gas technologies, we can achieve micro-leak identification at very high cycle rates, ensuring the safety compliance of every battery leaving the factory.

Industrial Vacuum System Integrity Monitoring

In industrial environments such as semiconductor processing and vacuum metallurgy, extremely high vacuum levels are key to process success. Metachip's sensors can be directly integrated into the front end of vacuum units to monitor atmospheric leakage into the chamber in real-time. Even during complex dynamic pumping processes, leakage sources can be precisely located, minimizing downtime for maintenance.

Customized Tracer Gas Integration Solutions

We provide not only sensor cores but also complete system integration solutions. Targeting different industry sealing shapes, internal volumes, and test pressures, we offer customized integrated modules for gas filling, monitoring, and tail gas treatment, helping OEMs quickly build their own high-precision leak detection equipment.

Digital Upgrade of Manufacturing Quality

By integrating leak detection data into the workshop's MES system in real-time, Metachip helps customers transform quality management from "post-inspection" to "process analysis." Through big data accumulation, customers can analyze potential trend defects in sealing processes, thereby proactively optimizing front-end assembly flows and improving overall line pass rates.
