Green Energy Storage

Focusing on the intrinsic safety of energy storage stations, providing cell-level thermal runaway early warning and full-dimensional environmental control applications.

Cell-level Thermal Runaway Warning
Energy Storage Station Environmental Monitoring
Early Detection of Electrolyte Leakage
Smart Energy Storage Fire Linkage

Millisecond-level Cell Thermal Runaway Warning

Battery thermal runaway is the greatest safety hazard for energy storage stations. The embedded MEMS sensors developed by Metachip can monitor abnormal surges of characteristic gases (such as CO, H2, VOC) inside the battery pack in real-time. Since the sensor is closer to the heat source and has an extremely fast response speed, it can issue an alarm 5-10 minutes before visible smoke occurs in the battery, winning precious time for the system to cut off the current and start fire protection.

Comprehensive Environmental Control for Large-scale Energy Storage Stations

For megawatt-level container energy storage stations and industrial and commercial energy storage cabinets, Metachip has created a full-space environmental perception network. The core of the solution is the deployment of MC-H2-Pro Composite Gas Detector (integrating H2/CO/VOCs detection), precisely installed in easy-accumulation areas at the top of battery clusters and ventilation dead spots, complemented by the MC-THS Intelligent Temperature and Humidity Sensing Terminal installed on the cabin ceiling.

Based on Metachip's unique carbon-based chip technology, the system possesses advantages such as resistance to silicon poisoning and long lifespan. It can alarm immediately when main characteristic gases are released during the early invisible smoke stage (Off-gas Release) of thermal runaway. Through millisecond-level linkage with the EMS local controller, it automatically triggers primary ventilation and secondary fire suppression, "nipping the accident in the bud" and building an insurmountable safety firewall for large-scale energy storage assets.

Electrolyte Leakage Monitoring and Prevention

Electrolyte leakage not only leads to a decline in battery performance but is also a precursor to fire. Metachip's high-sensitivity VOC sensors can monitor specific ester compounds volatilized from the electrolyte at extremely low concentrations. This specificity in detection capability can effectively distinguish between environmental impurity interference and achieve extremely early identification of leakage.

Smart Fire Protection and Emergency Linkage Upgrade

We deeply integrate sensing data with automatic fire sprinklers and perfluorohexanone suppression systems. Through precise positioning alarms, the fire protection system can achieve "point-to-point" precise spraying, minimizing the impact of accidents on adjacent battery clusters, reducing losses to a minimum, and achieving intrinsically safe energy storage operations.
