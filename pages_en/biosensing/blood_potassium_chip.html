Portable Blood Potassium Detection Chip

Using the Ion-Sensitive Field-Effect Transistor (ISFET) detection method, it is a domestic pioneer in the IVD industry, initiating a new era of point-of-care (POCT) rapid blood potassium testing.

  • Wide linear range (100μM - 100mM), covering clinical common concentration ranges
  • Excellent selectivity, effectively suppressing interference from other ions to ensure accuracy
  • High sensitivity, precisely capturing subtle changes in blood potassium concentration
  • Short response time (< 60 seconds), enabling immediate testing and rapid diagnosis

Product Details

The portable blood potassium detection chip uses the Ion-Sensitive Field-Effect Transistor (ISFET) detection method, which is a pioneer in the IVD industry. This product offers easy operation, rapid testing, higher sensitivity, better selectivity, and a wider linear range.

Combined with our portable biochemical analyzer, it enables rapid point-of-care testing of blood potassium concentration, which is significant for predicting outcomes by monitoring hyperkalemia. This chip is especially suitable for clinical scenarios such as Emergency, ICU, and Nephrology where rapid blood potassium data is essential.

Product Advantages

Easy Operation

No complex sample pretreatment required. One-touch operation allows medical staff to master it quickly, reducing operational errors.

Rapid Response

Complete testing within 60 seconds, gaining precious time for critically ill patients and enhancing clinical efficiency.

Precise Testing

Accuracy and precision both ≤ 5%. Reliable test results provide solid data support for clinical diagnosis and treatment decisions.

Main Applications

CSF Testing

Used for electrolyte analysis in CSF for patients in Neurology and Neurosurgery, aiding in the diagnosis of CNS diseases and metabolic abnormalities.

Urine Testing

Applied in monitoring potassium ion concentration in Nephrology and Urology to assess kidney function and electrolyte balance.

Serum Testing

Widely used in Emergency, ICU, and Cardiology for rapid serum potassium testing, facilitating timely detection and treatment of hyper/hypokalemia.
