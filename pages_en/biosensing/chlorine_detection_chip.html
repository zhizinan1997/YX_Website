Residual Chlorine Detection Chip

An innovative water quality monitoring core device based on micro-nano fabrication technology and electrochemical principles, providing reliable data support for smart water systems.

  • Miniaturized design (6*4mm), easy to integrate into various portable or online monitoring devices
  • Maintains excellent linearity and sensitivity within a wide range of 0-20mg/L
  • Obtain stable residual chlorine concentration readings within 30 seconds
  • Optimized for complex water environments with strong ion selectivity and anti-interference capability

Product Details

Metachip's residual chlorine detection chip is a high-performance water quality detection core chip designed and manufactured using advanced micro-nano fabrication (MEMS) processes combined with electrochemical detection principles. It breaks the volume limitations of traditional residual chlorine detection equipment, featuring miniaturization, low power consumption, and maintenance-free characteristics.

The chip is specially optimized for the monitoring needs of domestic drinking water and industrial water, maintaining high sensitivity and stability over a wide pH range of 4-9. It effectively solves the drift problem in long-term online monitoring and is an ideal choice for building a new generation of smart water IoT terminals.

Product Advantages

Highly Integrated & Miniaturized

Only 6*4mm, easily embedded into various handheld instruments and compact online monitoring probes.

Wide Range & High Precision

Covers a 0-20mg/L range with a resolution of up to 0.01mg/L, meeting monitoring needs from micro to high concentrations.

Excellent Repeatability

Repeatability error is controlled within 1%, ensuring the reliability of every measurement and reducing calibration frequency.

Main Applications

Water Treatment Plant Monitoring

Used for real-time online monitoring of residual chlorine in tap water and pipe network ends to ensure city water disinfection effectiveness and safety.

Swimming Pool Water quality Management

Widely used in swimming pools and SPAs for monitoring disinfectant concentration to prevent over-disinfection or under-disinfection.

Drinking Water Safety

Suitable for community direct drinking water stations, home water purifiers, and secondary water tanks, guarding the "last mile" of water safety.
