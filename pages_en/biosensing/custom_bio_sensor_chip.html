Customization Workflow

We have established a standardized and efficient chip customization workflow to ensure every step from requirement alignment to final delivery precisely meets your research or commercial needs.

1. Requirement Inquiry

Communicate detection targets, scenarios, sensitivity requirements, and expected costs; sign NDA.

2. Solution Design

Provide layout design, process selection, and packaging plans; output feasibility report.

3. Wafer Prototyping

Enter 4-inch MEMS production line for photolithography, deposition, etching, etc.

4. Packaging & Testing

Complete dicing, wire bonding, microfluidic integration, and electrical validation.

5. Delivery & Acceptance

Deliver chip samples, test reports, and manuals; provide after-sales technical support.
