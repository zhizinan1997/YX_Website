Carbon-Based Biosensing Platform

A versatile biomolecular interaction analysis and detection platform built on carbon-based field-effect transistor (Carbon-FET) technology.

  • Fast response, real-time monitoring of biomolecular binding dynamics
  • Ultra-high sensitivity, supporting direct detection of low-abundance samples
  • Excellent biocompatibility, suitable for proteins, nucleic acids, and cells
  • Fully compatible with microfluidic channel designs for automated sampling and analysis
  • Wafer-level manufacturing process ensuring high consistency between chips
Custom Development Inquiry

Product Details

Metachip's carbon-based biosensing platform employs semiconductor engineering methods to mass-manufacture high-uniformity Carbon-FET arrays. This platform offers core advantages such as miniaturization, low noise, and high sensitivity. It not only supports standardized biochip products but can also be customized in size and surface functionalization based on specific customer needs.

As an open technical platform, its manufacturing process is fully compatible with automated packaging lines and seamlessly integrates with Metachip's independently developed biosensing workstations and industrial-grade semiconductor analyzers (such as wafer-level testing supported by probe stations), providing powerful underlying hardware support for scientific exploration and IVD reagent development.

Product Advantages

Underlying Technology Breakthrough

Utilizing the卓越 electrical properties of carbon-based materials to break through the sensitivity bottlenecks of silicon sensors, achieving single-molecule level detection potential.

Highly Customizable

Provides a full-process customization service from chip structure design and surface biological modification to packaging formats, satisfying diverse application scenarios.

System Compatibility

Seamlessly integrates microfluidic systems and electrical readout circuits, providing standardized interfaces for building fully automated Lab-on-a-Chip systems.

Main Applications

Emergency POCT

As a core sensing component, it empowers a new generation of handheld/desktop medical diagnostic equipment, achieving rapid quantitative analysis of cardiac markers and inflammatory factors.

Gene Screening

Using the charge-sensing principle, it directly reads nucleic acid hybridization signals without labeling, significantly reducing the time and cost for gene sequencing and pathogen screening.

Food Safety

Targeting small targets like pesticide/veterinary drug residues and biotoxins, highly sensitive portable detection cards are developed to ensure food chain safety from farm to table.
