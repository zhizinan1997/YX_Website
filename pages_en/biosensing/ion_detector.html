Ion Detector

Metachip's ion detector enables real-time on-site analysis for rapid detection of ions such as sodium, potassium, lead, and hydrogen.

  • High current detection precision (1nA) for capturing weak signals
  • Wide range: 1nA-30μA, adaptable to various concentrations
  • Bluetooth communication for easy mobile device connection
  • Independent calibration for 8 channels, supporting simultaneous testing of 4 analytes
  • Waterproof design, submersible in solutions
  • Quick plug-and-play linkage
Schedule a Demo

Product Details

The ion detector is a portable, fast, and precise test device using ion-selective electrode technology. It enables real-time on-site analysis for rapid detection of various ions (sodium, potassium, lead, hydrogen ions, etc.).

This equipment is widely applicable in environmental monitoring, food safety, water testing, agriculture, and healthcare, helping users quickly assess ion concentrations in the environment to provide data support for decision-making.
