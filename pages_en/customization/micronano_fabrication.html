Micro-nano Fabrication Services

Providing full-chain MEMS and semiconductor micro-nano fabrication services from design and simulation to processing and testing, relying on a professional Class 100 cleanroom manufacturing platform to accelerate your research innovation and product launch.

  • 4-inch wafer full-process compatibility, supporting customized processing for non-standard sizes
  • High-precision lithography process, with line accuracy of 1μm and overlay accuracy of 0.5μm
  • Complete thin film deposition capabilities, covering precious metals such as Ti/Pd/Au and oxide dielectrics
  • Efficient 7×24 production response, significantly shortening pilot and R&D cycles
  • Team of senior micro-nano process experts, providing one-on-one process route optimization consulting

服务详情

Metachip微纳加工服务平台基于公司先进的百级无尘车间，配备了接触式光刻机、电子束蒸发台、磁控溅射仪、反应离子刻蚀机等全套微纳加工设备。我们不仅提供标准的器件制造服务，更专注于为高校、研究所及科技企业提供复杂的定制化工艺Solutions。

Our scope of service covers MEMS sensor processing, biomicrofluidic chip manufacturing, carbon nanotube FET device construction, and patterning of various micro-nano structures. We support flexible cooperation models from single-step processes (such as lithography, coating) to full-process device fabrication, helping customers solve process problems from laboratory principle prototypes to engineering mass production.

Core Advantages

Professional Process Team

Led by several senior engineers with semiconductor manufacturing backgrounds, we deeply understand device physics mechanisms and can optimize design layouts and process flows for customers to improve yields.

Precision Manufacturing Capability

Equipped with imported high-precision processing equipment, we ensure high fidelity of pattern transfer and high density of film growth, meeting the stringent requirements of carbon-based chips and high-end MEMS devices.

Agile Delivery Cycle

A mature production line management system supports 24/7 continuous operation. We accelerate turnover through "green channels" for urgent orders, significantly shortening R&D iteration time.

Fabrication Capability Coverage

Carbon-based Electronic Devices

Specialized CNT-FET manufacturing process to achieve transistor arrays with high ON/OFF ratios and low subthreshold swings.

Biosensor Chips

Integrating microfluidic channels and electrochemical electrodes for DNA detection, immunoassays, and cell culture.

MEMS Microstructures

Release and etching of cantilever, micro-hotplate, and micro-cavity structures, suitable for pressure, gas, and flow sensors.
