Custom Instrument Development

End-to-end instrument customization services covering sensor bottom-level packaging, detector machine manufacturing, industrial design, and structural development, helping customers build differentiated high-end detection equipment

  • Complete machine definition and development capabilities, supporting handheld, fixed, and wearable devices
  • Deeply integrated sensor packaging technology to improve the overall response and anti-interference of the instrument
  • Professional Industrial Design (ID) and Mechanical Design (MD), balancing aesthetics and protection levels
  • Mature embedded software and hardware platforms for rapid data collection, display, and transmission
  • Strict quality testing and calibration processes to ensure final products meet industrial and safety standards

服务全景

Metachip的定制仪器仪表服务依托我们在传感器芯片领域的垂直整合能力，为客户提供“芯片+算法+硬件+结构”的一站式Solutions。我们不仅提供核心探测部件的制造，更延伸至整机Products的设计与组装，帮助客户降低研发门槛，缩短上市周期。

Whether it's an industrial online monitoring monitor that requires special explosion-proof design, or a wearable safety alarm that pursues extreme lightness, our R&D team can tailor a complete set of design solutions from appearance ID to internal PCBA based on customers' specific needs, and provide complete supply chain support from small batch trial production to large-scale delivery.

Our Advantages

Chip-level Customization

Unlike ordinary system integrators, we have our own underlying sensor chip manufacturing and packaging production lines, defining instrument performance from the source.

Full-stack R&D Strength

Our team is equipped with structural, hardware, embedded software, and upper computer development engineers, collaborating seamlessly and avoiding compatibility risks caused by sub-contracted development.

Flexible Manufacturing System

Adapt to the "small batch, multi-variety" characteristics of instrument customization, providing flexible production scheduling and strict quality control to ensure delivery quality.

Development Case Directions

Portable/Handheld Detectors

Low-power circuit design and ergonomic shells, suitable for mobile inspection and rapid field analysis.

Fixed/Online Monitoring Analyzers

Industrial-grade explosion-proof housings and standard signal outputs to meet the stringent requirements of continuous online monitoring.

Wearable Safety Equipment

Miniaturized and lightweight design, integrating sensing functions into helmets, work clothes, or wristbands.

Development Service Process

Requirement Analysis Communicate application scenarios, determine target gas, range, accuracy, size, and budget requirements
Solution Design Chip selection, circuit schematic design, appearance ID design, structural stacking design
Sample Prototype PCB prototyping, hand-board model making (3D printing/CNC), software and hardware coordination, initial calibration
Testing & Verification High and low temperature aging, environmental reliability testing, electromagnetic compatibility (EMC) pre-test, accuracy verification
Small Batch Trial Production Mold opening (if needed), trial production review, tool and fixture production, production process document (SOP) output
Mass Production Delivery Mass production, final factory calibration, accompanying inspection report, after-sales technical support
Certification Assistance Assist customers in submitting for inspection, and provide necessary rectification suggestions and technical document support
