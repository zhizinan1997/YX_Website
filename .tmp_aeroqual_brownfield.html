Brownfield Redevelopment

Exceed environmental and human health standards by investing in a premium all-in-one brownfield remediation air monitoring system.

Increasing urban regeneration represents an enormous commercial opportunity for responsible site remediators. Protecting workers and surrounding communities from air pollution and dust is an essential part of any brownfield remediation project. We’ve provided effective air monitoring solutions on hundreds of air pollution and dust remediation projects around the world.

From near-reference, simultaneous measurement of multiple particle fractions and gas pollutants via the Aeroqual AQS 1, to the Dust Sentry, and the connected, handheld Ranger, our brownfield remediation air monitoring solutions deliver accurate real-time data you can trust.

Safeguard workers with a robust brownfield remediation plan

Eliminate unnecessary site visits and protect worker safety with our remote air monitoring solutions. Access real-time data even when off-site and receive automatic alerts if emissions exceed safe levels with our Aeroqual OneView remediation monitoring software. Pause operations and swiftly deploy emissions control measures as needed, without exposing workers to undue risk.

Save time with a single supplier for your air monitoring system

Streamline complex data collection and reporting for all relevant air pollution and dust remediation site emissions with a fully integrated fenceline air monitoring system. Accurately measure particulate matter, pollutant gases, and meteorological factors, accessing data through a single web-based software service.

Reassure and protect the local community with a best-in-class system

Reassure local communities by deploying state-of-the-art air quality monitoring technology that limits the impact of air pollution and dust on brownfield remediation sites. Share accurate real-time data with community stakeholders, demonstrating your commitment to safeguarding public health. Our data is backed by numerous independent studies from government and environmental organizations around the globe.

Build your reputation as a leader in safe and effective remediation

Meet industry demand for dust and air pollution remediation services that go beyond minimum regulatory requirements. Establish yourself as a leading site remediator by actioning effective emissions management powered by industry-leading technology. Execute your brownfield remediation air monitoring plan to the highest possible standard and create future business opportunities as urban regeneration projects continue to grow.
